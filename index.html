About this model

Dominica's 84 × 84 km terrain and satellite imagery are real, showing Morne Diablotins (1,447 m), Morne Trois Pitons (1,387 m), coastal towns, and the surrounding Caribbean Sea and Atlantic Ocean.

The time column runs through a 15-hour Category 5 hurricane passage inspired by Hurricane Maria's September 2017 landfall (265 km/h winds, 922 hPa central pressure): approaching outer squall bands, the violent front eyewall strike, a crystal-clear ~19 km stadium-effect eye passing directly over the island, 180° rear eyewall wind reversal, intense eyewall lightning, swirling storm-surge ocean foam, and mountain flash-flood runoff.

All stages share one deterministic clock: pausing freezes the vortex, and scrubbing reconstructs clouds, lightning, sea state, and watershed runoff without drift. Meteorological parameters are illustrative, not an operational forecast.
